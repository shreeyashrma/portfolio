About

Scientist. Innovator.
Industry-Ready.

From vaccine manufacturing floors in Nepal to genomic research labs at Clemson — a rare blend of regulated industry rigor and cutting-edge science.

I am a doctoral researcher in Animal and Veterinary Sciences at Clemson University with a deep focus on microbiology, molecular biology, and translational research relevant to the biotechnology industry. My work spans microbial genomics, gut microbiome modulation, host-pathogen interactions, and vaccine efficacy — all with direct applications in biotech product development and quality assurance.

Prior to my doctoral studies, I spent a year as a Research Associate at BIOVAC Nepal, working within GMP-aligned vaccine manufacturing environments. I conducted sterility, potency, and stability testing, collaborated with QA/QC teams, and led a U.S. Department of State Biosecurity Project — sharpening my regulatory mindset and cross-functional communication skills.

I am a fellow of Clemson's competitive Accelerate to Industry (A2i) program, purpose-built to prepare graduate researchers for leadership roles in biotech and life sciences. I am actively pursuing opportunities in R&D, quality, or translational research.
